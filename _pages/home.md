I am **Jiacheng (Richard) Zhuo**, a Master student at the **University of California, Irvine (UCI)**, majoring in **Computer Software Engineering**.

My research interests include:
- Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Personal Informatics & Self-Tracking
- Mobile & Ubiquitous Health (mHealth) with foucs on Mental and Physcial Health
- Software Engineering for Health Technologies


I currently work with teams at the **UCI School of Nursing** on designing and developing **mobile health systems for maternal hypertension**, focusing on wearable data integration, daily self-tracking, personalized education resource and  behavior-aware feedback to support patient wellbeing.

My long-term goal is to design technologies that **help people track, understand, and improve their health and wellbeing** by integrating HCI methods, personal informatics theory, and health technology design. 

I aim to extend this work beyond **post-injury recovery** to broader areas such as **chronic condition management** and **aging-related care**, with a particular focus on supporting underserved, low-income, and marginalized populations through accessible and equitable digital health solutions.
