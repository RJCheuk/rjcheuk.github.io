## Overview
The **Happy Hearts Mobile System** is a modular mobile health intervention designed for high-risk perinatal women.  
It integrates **education**, **daily check-ins**, **wearable data**, **personalized feedback**, and **clinician communication** into a unified platform used by research teams at the UCI School of Nursing.

## My Role  
**Research Engineer & UX Study Designer (Jun 2025 – Present)**

- Designed patient-facing mobile interfaces (React Native + Expo)  
- Developed Spring Boot backend with JWT-secured APIs & role-based access control  
- Built clinician-facing dashboards for participant monitoring and workflow support  
- Modeled behavioral engagement metrics and alert escalation workflows  
- Prepared usability study protocols, scenarios, and evaluation metrics  
- Defined data pipelines and secure storage workflows for pilot deployment
