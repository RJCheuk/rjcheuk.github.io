## Overview
This project is part of my developing **PhD research direction** on personalized recovery support.  
Inspired by my own ACL rehabilitation, it investigates how mobile technologies can help individuals:

- Track recovery progress  
- Reflect on physical + emotional states  
- Understand long-term healing trajectories  
- Develop self-awareness around habits and behavior change  

## Research Goals
- Support **dual recovery dimensions**: physical + psychological  
- Improve long-term adherence and reflection  
- Enable personalized feedback loops for rehabilitation  
- Empower patients through meaningful self-tracking data
